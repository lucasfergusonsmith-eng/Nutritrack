AI FOOD SCANNER

Food

Take a photo. NUTRITRACK identifies the food, estimates portions and shows your macros automatically.

How the scanner works

AI estimate
① Check the sceneFirst checks whether food is actually visible and whether the photo is usable.
② Focus on the mealThe intended food area is prioritised so background objects are less likely to be treated as food.
③ Identify + estimateRecognises separate foods, estimates portions and shows calories, protein, carbs and fat automatically.
④ Rescan if neededIf the prediction looks wrong, press Rescan and the app runs another prediction instead of making you start again.

A real production scanner should combine vision AI with a verified nutrition database. A photograph cannot reliably measure exact grams, oils or hidden ingredients, so estimates should show confidence and allow corrections.
